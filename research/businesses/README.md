# ListPak Business Research Package

This directory contains research-only manual-entry material for the ListPak business and directory section. It does not insert or modify any database record, and it does not modify application code or existing listings.

## Main file

Open [`high-search-pakistan-businesses.md`](./high-search-pakistan-businesses.md). It contains 46 selected Pakistani business and directory entities across banking, insurance, telecom, internet, airlines, travel agencies, hotels, restaurants, hospitals, universities, schools, real estate, software, courier/logistics, e-commerce, apparel, automobiles, transport, recruitment, and utilities.

Each candidate includes the proposed business name, ListPak category and subcategory, city/location, address and contact-data instructions, official website, social-profile guidance, description, services, hours guidance, search intent, relevant keywords, qualitative SEO opportunity, research justification, and source URLs. Fields marked “verify” are intentionally not guessed; use the official business, branch, or contact page at the time of manual entry.

## Recommended order of manual entry

Start with the highest-intent entities that have complete official location and contact data: HBL, Meezan Bank, Jazz, PTCL, PIA, Aga Khan University Hospital, NUST, LUMS, Zameen.com, TCS, Leopards Courier, Daraz, Toyota Indus, and SNGPL. Then add the remaining entities after checking duplicate names, official domains, city/category values, and branch-level information.

National brands should not automatically receive one generic listing when searchers need a nearby branch, campus, hotel, hospital, dealership, courier office, or franchise. Create branch records only when the official locator provides a stable address, phone, hours, and map link. Keep every new record pending and unverified until your normal review and claim process is complete.

## Important safeguards

Do not copy third-party data-broker information, personal contact details, unverified reviews, unsupported ratings, or copyrighted images. Start records with `rating: 0`, `reviewCount: 0`, and `reviews: []`. Leave contact fields blank until the official business source confirms them. If an identical or near-identical ListPak listing already exists, enrich or claim it through the legitimate workflow rather than creating a duplicate.

## Research date and freshness

The research was performed on 23 August 2026 using current search-result discovery and official public business sources. Contact numbers, addresses, opening hours, branch counts, routes, services, and ownership can change. Recheck every operational field at manual-entry time.
