## Executive summary

This file contains **24 current public job opportunities** selected from live search-result pages and publicly accessible job listings. They cover software engineering, frontend/backend/full-stack development, data entry, virtual assistance, customer support, digital marketing, graphic design, accounting, growth, teaching, tutoring, internships, and remote work. The listings were selected because their source pages showed a current or recent discovery date, a named employer, a Pakistan-relevant location, and a direct or identifiable public application path.

The list is intentionally not a list of invented job templates. It is a manual-entry research queue. Each item must be re-opened before entering because job listings can close, change, or be replaced. If the live source no longer shows the role, do not enter it. If the source has no stable deadline, use the source’s current availability only after checking the listing at entry time; do not invent an expiry date.

Current Google-visible search patterns strongly favor role-plus-location and remote-intent queries such as **frontend developer jobs Pakistan**, **backend developer jobs Lahore**, **software engineer jobs Islamabad**, **data entry jobs Karachi**, **digital marketing jobs Lahore**, **remote jobs Pakistan**, **work from home jobs Pakistan**, **accountant jobs Lahore**, **online teacher jobs Pakistan**, and **call center jobs Karachi**. Current search-result pages show Indeed, Rozee.pk, LinkedIn, and Mustakbil as the main competing job-portal surfaces. Search visibility is not a guarantee that ListPak will rank; ListPak needs unique, fresh, structured, internally linked detail pages and a reliable expiry process.

## Research and safety rules

Only public business and job information was used. No private personal data was collected. Employer names are retained because they are part of the public job listing. Job descriptions and requirements below are concise rewrites of public listing content rather than copied full advertisements.

Before manual entry, confirm all of the following from the live source: job is still accepting applications; employer name is unchanged; city/remote status is correct; salary is still displayed; application URL works; requirements are current; and the source has not marked the job expired or removed it. Do not reuse a listing when the source page redirects to an expired result or to a generic search page without the vacancy.

For a new ListPak record, use `status: pending`, `verified: false`, `isFeatured: false`, `rating` is not applicable, and do not imply that ListPak is the employer. Do not copy the source site’s tracking or referral parameters unless needed for attribution, and do not publish a job without a working application path.

## Current competitor and SERP observations

Indeed currently exposes searchable Pakistan result pages for remote backend/frontend development, data entry, online student work, digital marketing, and related role clusters. Its result pages show employer, location, salary in some cases, job type, snippets, and direct application links. Rozee.pk exposes employer, career-level, experience, salary, city, and a large location taxonomy, and its current search results surfaced recent accountant and content-management listings. LinkedIn and Mustakbil are important competing surfaces for Pakistan professional and job discovery, but many LinkedIn results require login or redirect to an external job page.

The main content gap for ListPak is not merely the absence of generic role pages. The opportunity is a fresh, Pakistan-specific job index that combines **role + city + employment type + remote status + employer + skills + application freshness**, with one canonical detail page per active vacancy and automatic retirement or noindex handling after expiration. ListPak should avoid creating one page for every keyword variation when the page has no unique job inventory.

## Exact ListPak field mapping

Use the existing `JobItem` shape and the existing post-job workflow. The current form accepts or derives the following fields:

```text
title: verified source title
company: verified employer name
city: primary verified city or Pakistan for genuine nationwide/remote roles
cities: [primary city, additional verified cities only]
category: existing ListPak category such as Technology & IT, Finance & Banking, Education & Training, Media, PR & Advertising, Administration & Office, Customer Service & BPO, Retail & E-commerce, or Healthcare & Medical
type: Full-time | Part-time | Contract | Remote | Hybrid | Internship
employmentType: same as type unless source states a more specific value
salary: exact publicly stated salary or "Not stated"; never estimate
experience: exact stated experience or "Not stated"
education: exact stated education or "Not stated"
skills: verified skill list
vacancies: exact number if stated, otherwise 1 only if the source clearly presents one opening
deadline: exact public deadline, otherwise "Not stated — verify source"
joiningDate: only if stated; otherwise "Not stated"
workingHours: only if stated; otherwise "Not stated"
shiftType: Day, Evening, Night, Flexible, or "Not stated" based on source
benefits: only listed benefits; otherwise []
postedDate: source posting date if shown; otherwise discovery date plus verification note
description: concise factual rewrite, not copied full text
responsibilities: concise factual rewrite
requirements: concise factual rewrite
preferredQualifications: only if explicitly stated
applicationWebsite: stable direct application or careers URL
applicationEmail: public application email only if the source provides one
applicationMethod: website, email, both, or listpak
applicationUrl: stable direct application URL
verified: false until manually reviewed
isFeatured: false
status: pending
```

## Expiration and SEO/product issue found in the current architecture

The current `lib/job-service.ts` maps `deadline` into job records and preserves pending/approved/rejected status, but the public `getAllJobs()` path filters status and does not visibly filter jobs by an expired deadline. The service also falls back to in-memory mock jobs when Firestore is empty or unavailable, catches Firestore write errors and still returns a local job object, and `getJobBySlug()` can fall back to the first cached job for an unknown identifier. These behaviors create a real risk of stale listings, false-success submissions, and duplicate or misleading detail pages.

This file does not change that architecture. For the manual workflow, every job must be checked before entry and rechecked before publication. Before building more inventory, the product should define a clear expiration policy: use a real date where the employer states one, otherwise require periodic human confirmation, remove expired jobs from the sitemap, and prevent expired detail pages from returning indexable 200 responses. Any future architecture change should be planned separately and tested against the existing admin workflow.

## Manual-entry and verification checklist

For every candidate, open the source URL on the day of entry and confirm the vacancy remains open. Copy the exact employer, title, city, employment type, salary, requirements, deadline, and application URL. Search ListPak by normalized title plus employer, employer name, application domain, and city before saving. If an identical job exists, do not duplicate it; update or enrich it through the legitimate workflow if appropriate.

Keep the record pending until admin review. Confirm the job detail URL uses the generated slug, the employer and location render correctly, application links work, the page includes valid `JobPosting` structured data only when required fields are truly present, and the job is not included in the sitemap after it expires. Check internal links from the jobs hub, category pages, city pages, and relevant employer/company pages. Never publish an expired job, an employer-less job, a salary that was inferred, or a job whose application link no longer works.
